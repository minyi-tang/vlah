These are suggestions, not rules.

- Prefer an idea you can measure over one you can only argue for.
- Isolate a change when it helps explain a result; combine changes when the idea
  depends on their interaction.
- Search the blackboard before you submit a candidate: someone may have tried it.
- When an idea fails, record a dead_end whose evidence cites the candidate it
  tested (candidates/<hash>/...), so nobody runs it again. A clean negative result
  is useful evidence.
- Use surprise for anything unexpected, even before you know what it means.
