are in CONTEXT.md, in your working directory. Read it to the end: the herd appends
notices there, such as an interrupt or a restart note.

Your working directory holds:
- target/: the system you are improving, checked out at the task's baseline;
- blackboard/: a read-only copy of the shared blackboard, refreshed every minute;
- CONTEXT.md: your start pack.
