# DECISIONS

Choices the spec (*Herd Testbed* §11; in this repo also issue #10) left open, plus every place we departed from it and why.
One row each: what, why, and how expensive it is to change. Nothing here changes the §11.5 scorecard
definitions or the three anti-cheat rules.

## Problem A: simulator and sites

The precheck failed on the spec's numbers. Each change below was tuned on separate seeds
(1001–1020) so the hidden dev seeds were not tuned on, then confirmed by the 20-dev-seed precheck.
Before/after numbers are under "Precheck history" below.

| # | Decision | Why | Cost to change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Simulated horizon 144 h on the slow tier, not 48 h (`sites/common.yaml: horizon_s`); the fast tier stays 24 h (A13). | Per-seed noise was too high for precheck row 1 once the composability retune (A15) spread the gain over all five sites. 96 h was the first step; 144 h cuts sd by about √1.5 more. | Low (one number); a 20-seed slow evaluation takes about 1.5× longer. |
| A13 | The fast tier (`tb fast`, public seeds) simulates 24 h; the slow tier and `final_check` simulate the slow horizon (A1: 144 h). `baseline_scores.json`, calibration's `baseline_fast` and every `fast_run` baseline come from the same 24 h trace as `tb fast`. | Decided for the user (who was away) by the session "Research herd 测试台搭建", 2026-10-22: the local test should be noisy, as the spec intends. The trap still beats the baseline locally on 5/5 seeds, but its site-2 p99 regression is also visible locally on 5/5, so a careful agent can still spot it. Open: a shorter fast trace or an intermittent trap would be needed to hide it. | Low |
| A14 | **Deviation from 11.6 (the wait definition and the site-4 mechanism), flagged for the user to confirm.** 11.6 defines wait as "一个任务所有排队区间之和" and puts the start-up cost on run time. With the optional mechanism `startup.counts_as_wait` (site 4 only, A5), a cold start instead adds its overhead (720 s) to the job's wait, while the job starts at once and holds its cores only for its run time. The overhead is therefore a pure score penalty, not a delay that anything waits behind, and the score formula holds only literally: it is still the mean of `wait`, but `wait` at site 4 is no longer just queueing time. This penalty is what makes packing worth z = 162 on its own site. Alternative, literal to 11.6: `counts_as_wait: false` (the overhead on run time). Packing then acts only through load, about 0.3% of the gain before the retune, which fails precheck row 6's 8% floor; site 4 would need another way to make packing pay. | Proposed by this session during the composability retune (A15), not decided by the user; flagged on review by the session "Research herd 测试台搭建". | Medium (changes what site 4 measures) |
| A15 | Composability retune (user decision 2026-10-22). On 10 tuning seeds (1001–1010, never the hidden ones) the five ingredients alone took 13–33% of the gain, the best pair 56%, the best triple about 80%; before: admission alone 58%, admission + backfill 93%, packing 0.3%. Changes: A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, R3, R4. Hidden-seed numbers are under Precheck history. | So that one agent who finds the two obvious ingredients cannot already reach the 70% capture target. **Acceptance check failed (open, user decision).** The 30-min single-arm LLM smoke run `r-llm-smoke-retune` (copilot, claude-opus-5.5, `slow_seeds` 5, `visible_delay_s` 120) reached 84% capture at 6.6 min with three ingredients (admission, locality, packing) and 97% at 22 min with four (est_order never used). The agent mapped each public site description to its fix in one pass, so share caps on single ingredients and pairs do not stop it. The remaining lever is to make the mechanisms less visible (vaguer site descriptions, hidden generator parameters), which also closes the local-judge-copy route. | Medium |
| A2 | Site 1: the big user (60% of jobs) submits 1-core jobs (lognormal, mean 20 min, σ 0.4); the other users submit 2/4/8-core jobs (lognormal mean 40 min, σ 0.5). Load 0.75. The spec had Pareto α=1.5 for everyone at 0.85. | With Pareto, per-seed noise swamped backfill and the backfill/fairshare conflict did not appear. This follows the spec's own description of the conflict: strict fairness holds back the big user's short jobs, so backfill has nothing to fill gaps with. | Medium (re-tune the conflict) |
| A3 | Site 2: peaks 2.3× (spec: 5×), durations lognormal mean 20 min σ 0.3. | Admission at site 2 took 54–58% of the whole gain by itself (A15), and site 2 dominated the score's noise. | Low |
| A4 | Site 3: load 0.5, all jobs 4-core, duration σ_log 0.3, off-rack penalty 2× (spec: 0.8, mixed sizes, 0.8, 3×). | With the spec's values the first-fit baseline is past saturation; mixed and 8-core jobs made site 3 too noisy for precheck row 1. | Low |
| A5 | Site 4: 60 users, 1–3 min jobs, load 0.55. A cold start (the user hasn't run on that machine in 20 min) adds a 12-minute wait and does not hold the cores (`counts_as_wait`, see A14). Spec: 20 users, 5–15 min, 30 s added to the run time, load 0.7. | With a cold start that only lengthens the job, packing mattered only near overload: tiny (0.3% of the gain) or very noisy. Counting it as waiting makes packing's gain direct and steady (own-site z ≈ 180). | Medium (a mechanism change, public in site4.yaml) |
| A6 | Sites 5 and 6: load 0.8, all jobs whole-machine (8 cores). | Whole-machine jobs keep backfill out of sites 5 and 6, so each ingredient's gain stays at its own site (A15). | Low |
| A7 | After arrivals stop, the simulation drains for at most 24 h. Jobs still queued count their wait up to that point. | Bounds runtime for policies that starve jobs. | Low |
| A8 | Runaway guard: more than 5M hook calls at one site voids the evaluation (deterministic). | A starving policy, such as strict non-work-conserving fairshare, otherwise ran for hours. The baseline makes 36k–333k calls per site. | Low |
| A9 | The child process runs a whole scheduling pass per request: one round trip per pass, with each hook call timed separately on CPU time. The parent re-checks every decision. | One round trip per hook call made a seed take minutes. | Medium |
| A10 | Policy isolation: `socket` is blocked inside the host process. On macOS, `sandbox-exec` also denies network and reads of every hidden path. On Linux, `unshare -rn` is used when `TB_SANDBOX=strict`. | Spec: no network, cwd is a temp dir. Anti-cheat is not the goal (§0). | Low |
| A11 | Score per seed = −(mean over sites of mean wait). The aggregate score is the mean over seeds; p99 is pooled over seeds per site (spec 11.6). The constraint tolerance is max(2% of the baseline's pooled p99, sd of the baseline's per-seed p99). | Spec 11.6. | — |
| A12 | The simulator also reports per-class waits (cores, estimated duration, big user or not) and mechanism counters (cold starts, off-rack placements, overloaded seconds). `map/gap.py` turns them into gap.md. | This is the opportunity map's first measurement script (D76). | Low |

## Problem A: reference, ingredients, probes

| # | Decision | Why | Cost to change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | The reference is one policy with a switch per ingredient (`reference/params.yaml`). Listing only the on-switches lets two variants merge key by key. | Low |
| R2 | Backfill = EASY backfill with a reservation for the blocked head. At sites with the overload mechanism (site 2) it runs strictly: only jobs that finish before the reservation. | Found during tuning: aggressive backfill breaks site 2's p99 on every seed (an unplanned second trap). | Low |
| R3 | Duration-estimate ordering with aging: anything waiting more than 60 min goes first (spec: 30 min). | R4 | trap_sjf = duration-estimate ordering with no aging, applied at every site (the reference's site skips do not apply to it). It breaks site 2's p99, not site 1's as the spec predicts. | Behaviour of our sites. The trap still voids on the slow tier, which is what the spec requires. | — |
| R5 | Fairshare (strict) = counted in running jobs. A user above 1.5× the per-user average is moved to the back and is not started while an under-share user waits. | R7 | The trap probe is a two-hour mini simulation on one machine: one long job and three short arrivals per minute. trap_sjf = est_order passes and the long job never starts in ≥90% of instances. | Spec 11.6. | — |

| R9 | Precheck row 5 (ours): each good ingredient alone meets the p99 constraint. | The integrator only takes constraint_ok results, so an ingredient that fails alone can only enter via another one. That would be a hidden dependency. | Low |

## Scorecard (interpretations of §11.5)

| # | Decision | Why | Cost to change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Worker-behaviour rows (breadth, dead-end re-hits, quota discipline, prediction error, tilt) use only worker events on the dev dataset. Runner, integrator, composer and slow-eval events are excluded. | Integrator re-measures are not worker submissions. | Low |
| S2 | Dead-end re-hits follows 11.5 literally: a sum over failed results (and dead_end writes) of the later submits with the same ingredient set, duplicates included. The number of distinct re-hitting submits, and the count excluding the empty set (no probe fired), are reported in the detail. | 11.5 is authoritative. An earlier version counted distinct submits; that changed the definition (review). | Low |
| S3 | Quota discipline and prediction error include duplicate submits (a duplicate's score is its original's). | 11.5 says "submit 中". An earlier version excluded them (review). | Low |
| S7 | Diagnostics show the testbed's own dev submissions (runner, integrator, composer), stale-target labels and agent restarts. | So nothing that spends evaluations is invisible. | Low |
| S4 | Prediction error needs at least 4 scored submits; otherwise "n/a". Halves are by submission order. | A median of one or two values is noise. | Low |
| S5 | Tilt: the running best starts at the baseline's slow score; only results that meet the constraint count; "1 CI width" is the triggering result's own CI. | §11.5 does not say. | Low |
| S6 | Generalization is signed (seen − unseen). With a CI width of 0 it is 0 if equal, else ±∞. | The stub problem has zero noise. | Low |
