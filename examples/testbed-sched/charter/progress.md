# Progress

Success is one integrated policy with an official score, sample count,
uncertainty and passing per-site constraints, plus enough evidence to explain
which changes helped and which did not. No measurements have been made for this
draft.

Track predictions against returned scores, gaps by site and job class, rejected
directions and interactions between changes. Preserve the lineage of the final
baseline and any unresolved audit or constraint failures.

There is no numeric score target or preregistered effect size here. The
maintainer chooses those for a future comparison; local UI feedback cannot be
reported as that comparison or as a pilot.
